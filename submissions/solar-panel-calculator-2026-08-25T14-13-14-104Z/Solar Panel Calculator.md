# Overview
A browser calculator for rough residential solar planning. It estimates system size, panel count, roof footprint, annual generation, and optional cost and simple payback from user-supplied electricity use, solar resource, panel, and price assumptions. It does not replace a site-specific installer design or quote.

**Entrepreneurship stage:**
Most useful during early feasibility and design work when a user is testing rough system assumptions.

**Scope of relevance:**
Works wherever users can supply local peak-sun-hours, electricity-use, panel, cost, and rate assumptions; outputs are not location-specific forecasts.
- Tags: #global

**Methodological approach:**
Quantitative scenario calculation using annual electricity use, peak sun hours, system losses, panel wattage, and optional cost inputs.

**Skill development:**
Helps users interpret electricity use, solar capacity, panel counts, roof area, and simple payback assumptions.
- Tags: #environmental-awareness #data-analysis-and-metrics

**Source and credibility:**
The page cites NLR and NREL PVWatts documentation, U.S. Department of Energy solar cost benchmarks, EIA electricity data, and NSRDB solar-resource data.
- Tags: #government-sources #research

**Prerequisites and requirements:**
Users need annual electricity use or a known DC system size, plus a local peak-sun-hours estimate. Optional cost outputs require local price and rate assumptions.
- Tags: #beginner-level

**Collaboration level:**
Designed for individual scenario planning; results can be shared with installers or other project participants for further review.
- Tags: #individual

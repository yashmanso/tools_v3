# Overview
WasteSource PH is an independent online directory for businesses and institutions finding regulated waste-service providers in the Philippines. Search by waste code, provider role, service and location, then review dated public-source evidence before contacting a provider. Historical registry records do not establish current authorization or service availability; WasteSource is not affiliated with DENR/EMB or listed providers.

___
# Dimensions

**Resource type:**
A free online provider-discovery tool and public-source directory for regulated waste services in the Philippines.
- Tags: #software-tool

**Objective:**
Help Philippine organizations shortlist regulated waste-service providers by waste code, service and location, and examine dated evidence before making inquiries.

**Target audience:**
Pollution Control Officers, compliance and facility teams, manufacturers, hospitals and procurement staff in Philippine businesses and institutions.
- Tags: #practitioners #smes #corporations

**Sustainability focus:**
Finding hazardous-waste transport, treatment, storage, disposal and recovery services using public-source records. No claim of measured waste diversion or environmental impact reduction.
- Tags: #environmental-sustainability

**Innovation type:**
Online information discovery supporting waste-service procurement and operational planning.

**Entrepreneurship stage:**
Useful during implementation and ongoing operations when an organization needs to identify waste-service options and review provider evidence.
- Tags: #implementation

**Scope of relevance:**
Philippines. Provider discovery can be narrowed by location, service and Philippine hazardous-waste code; actual service coverage must be confirmed with the provider.
- Tags: #national

**Methodological approach:**
Structures public-source provider records by waste code, role, service and geography, with dated source references and evidence limitations for preliminary due diligence.

**Skill development:**
Supports preliminary waste-code and source-evidence literacy while researching providers. Users still need to confirm current authorization, accepted waste scope and availability directly.

**Source and credibility:**
Independent Arkhe directory drawing on dated DENR/EMB registry records and other cited public sources, including provider websites. Historical records are identified as historical evidence and do not prove current authorization. No government affiliation or endorsement is claimed.
- Tags: #government-sources

**Prerequisites and requirements:**
Public browsing requires a web browser and internet connection, with no account needed. Knowing the waste stream or code helps narrow the search; regulatory and procurement decisions require independent checks.
- Tags: #beginner-level

**Collaboration level:**
An individual can research providers, while compliance, procurement and facility teams can use the same public source references during their review.
- Tags: #individual #team

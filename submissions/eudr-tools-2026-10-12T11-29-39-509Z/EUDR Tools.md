# Overview
EUDR Tools is a free browser-based workspace for teams preparing data for the EU Deforestation Regulation. It helps users check product scope and country risk, validate and repair GeoJSON, review plot-area rules, organise risk-assessment records, and prepare DDS reference information. It does not certify compliance, guarantee legality, or submit statements to the EU system. The service is available in 24 EU languages and can be used without an account.
